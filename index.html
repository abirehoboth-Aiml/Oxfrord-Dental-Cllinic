Common questions

Is treatment painful?

We use gentle techniques and local anaesthetic whenever needed. Tell us if you feel anxious and we’ll go at your pace.

How often should I visit?

Most people benefit from a check-up and cleaning every six months. We’ll suggest what suits you.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Larger treatments can be split into monthly payments, explained before you commit.

Can I come in for an emergency?

Call us and we’ll fit you in the same day wherever possible.
